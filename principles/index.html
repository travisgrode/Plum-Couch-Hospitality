The Host Reach Model

Scale the system. Protect the relationship.

Standardize the conditions that allow great relationships to happen more often. Scale the system, protect the relationship.

Seven principles

Seven principles for putting limited host attention where it creates the most value.

  • Know where PD creates value. Focus personalized service where it can influence player behavior and create profitable growth.
  • Be selective about who gets hosted. Set clear criteria for who warrants a host, including players with potential beyond their current play.
  • Protect host time and attention. Size books around visit frequency, workload, and the service you expect, not player counts alone.
  • Build structure that supports relationships. Give hosts clear coding, useful player information, feedback, and tools to manage their books.
  • Teach judgment, not just rules. Teach player value, profitability, reinvestment, and how to understand what each player values.
  • Measure what you want the host to do. Align goals and incentives with revenue, service, reach, and disciplined reinvestment. Coach consistently.
  • Earn the right to scale. Make the fundamentals work, then identify potential earlier and expand reach without diluting service.

Hosts change. Leaders change. The playbook stays.

This is the plumbing. I help properties build it, so the host program runs on a system instead of a few great personalities, and holds up through turnover.
